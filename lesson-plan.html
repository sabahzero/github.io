        <h2>Preparation/Planning</h2>

        <p><strong>ESL Course:</strong> English for Everyday Life</p>

        <p><strong>Level:</strong> Intermediate</p>

        <p><strong>Topic/Theme:</strong> Sustainable Living Practices</p>


        <h3>Objectives</h3>

        <ul>

            <li><strong>Listening:</strong> Students listen to a short teacher presentation and peer discussions to identify key information about sustainable practices.</li>

            <li><strong>Speaking:</strong> Students discuss sustainable practices in pairs and small groups using sentence frames.</li>

            <li><strong>Reading:</strong> Students read a 150-200 word text about sustainable practices and identify main ideas and supporting details.</li>

            <li><strong>Writing:</strong> Students complete a graphic organizer identifying sustainable practices and their benefits, and write an exit ticket response.</li>

        </ul>


        <h3>Bridging</h3>

        <p><strong>What background knowledge do the students already have?</strong></p>

        <p>Students come from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Many have prior knowledge of environmental issues such as recycling, pollution, or conservation.</p>


        <p><strong>What will you do to activate or link students' prior knowledge?</strong></p>

        <p>I will begin with a Think-Pair-Share activity asking students to discuss: "What does it mean to live sustainably?" This activates prior knowledge and allows students to share their own experiences.</p>


        <!-- Continue with all your lesson plan content -->


        <h3>Assessment</h3>

        <ul>

            <li><strong>Formative:</strong> Vocabulary sorting activity, oral participation, completed graphic organizer</li>

            <li><strong>Summative:</strong> Exit Ticket, observation and anecdotal notes</li>

        </ul>


        <h3>Closing</h3>

        <p>I will briefly recap the main points of the lesson, asking students to recall the key sustainable practices we discussed. Students will complete the exit ticket independently. I will preview the next lesson and introduce the upcoming project-based extension.</p>
