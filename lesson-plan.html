Model Lesson Plan

\ \
\ \

Preparation/Planning

\

ESL Course: English for Everyday Life

\

Level: Intermediate

\

Topic/Theme: Sustainable Living Practices

\ \

Objectives

\
    \
  • Listening: Students listen to a short teacher presentation and peer discussions to identify key information about sustainable practices.
    • \
  • Speaking: Students discuss sustainable practices in pairs and small groups using sentence frames.
    • \
  • Reading: Students read a 150-200 word text about sustainable practices and identify main ideas and supporting details.
    • \
  • Writing: Students complete a graphic organizer identifying sustainable practices and their benefits, and write an exit ticket response.
    • \
\ \

Bridging

\

What background knowledge do the students already have?

\

Students come from diverse cultural and linguistic backgrounds. Many have prior knowledge of environmental issues such as recycling, pollution, or conservation.

\ \

What will you do to activate or link students' prior knowledge?

\

I will begin with a Think-Pair-Share activity asking students to discuss: "What does it mean to live sustainably?" This activates prior knowledge and allows students to share their own experiences.

\ \ \ \

Assessment

\
    \
  • Formative: Vocabulary sorting activity, oral participation, completed graphic organizer
    • \
  • Summative: Exit Ticket, observation and anecdotal notes
    • \
\ \

Closing

\

I will briefly recap the main points of the lesson, asking students to recall the key sustainable practices we discussed. Students will complete the exit ticket independently. I will preview the next lesson and introduce the upcoming project-based extension.
